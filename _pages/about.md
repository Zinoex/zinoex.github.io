Frederik is a Research Associate at [Department of Computer Science, University of Oxford](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/) under the supervision of [Prof. Alessandro Abate](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/alessandro.abate/). Frederik completed his PhD in 2026 at [Delft Center for Systems and Control (DCSC), TU Delft](https://www.tudelft.nl/3me/over/afdelingen/delft-center-for-systems-and-control), supervised by [Dr. Luca Laurenti](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/staff/l.laurenti/?cHash=d00d764e03ebc8279dc6679ff1d65e18) and [Dr. Simeon Calvert](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/ceg/about-faculty/departments/transport-planning/staff/personal-pages/calvert-sc). His research lies at the intersection of **formal methods**, **learning-enabled systems**, and **cyber-physical systems (CPS)**, with a focus on providing rigorous correctness and guarantees for autonomous and safety-critical systems.
