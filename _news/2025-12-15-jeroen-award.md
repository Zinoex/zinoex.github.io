Honored to announce that **Jeroen Hagenus**, whom I supervised for his MSc thesis, has received the **Best MSc Thesis with AI Award** from Delft University of Technology in 2025.

His thesis, *"Robustness in Trajectory Prediction for Autonomous Vehicles,"* addresses the critical challenge of ensuring reliable trajectory prediction models in safety-critical autonomous driving applications. Jeroen is now continuing his research as a Doctoral researcher at the Delft Center for Systems and Control.

Congratulations, Jeroen!
