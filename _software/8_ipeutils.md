**ipeutils** is a collection of utilities for working with [Ipe](https://ipe.otfried.org/) figures, including styles, preambles, and custom ipelets.

## Scope

- Utilities for repetitive figure-editing tasks
- Colour schemes, including seaborn palettes and TU Delft-specific colours
- Ipelets for figure workflows, including configurable PNG export

## Notes

- Includes stylesheets and figure-building utilities used in day-to-day research workflows
